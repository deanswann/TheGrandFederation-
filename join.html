Membership

Join the Federation

The Grand Federation welcomes nations that want to take part in a growing regional community and help shape its future.

How to Join

  1. Open The Grand Federation on NationStates.
  2. Move your nation into the region.
  3. Read the regional rules and announcements.
  4. Introduce yourself and participate when you are ready.
  5. Active and trusted members may later be offered regional offices or delegated powers.

Go to The Grand Federation

What We Look For

There is no ideological test. The Federation values activity, reliability, respectful discussion, and a willingness to contribute to the regional community.
