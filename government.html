Regional Government

Government

The Grand Federation delegates practical powers to trusted members. Offices are meant to spread responsibility, reward participation, and keep the region active without weakening the authority of the Founder.

Regional Offices

Trusted players may be granted one or more regional powers depending on their role, activity, and level of responsibility.

ExecutiveHigh-level regional administration and day-to-day executive authority.
SuccessionAuthority connected to continuity of leadership and regional succession arrangements.
World AssemblyManagement and coordination of World Assembly-related regional activity.
AppearanceResponsibility for regional presentation, visual identity, and public-facing appearance.
Border ControlAuthority over admissions, removals, and regional security decisions where appropriate.
EmbassiesManagement of embassy relations with other regions.
CommunicationsRegional messages, announcements, dispatches, and public communication.
PollsCreation and management of regional polls and member consultation.
Principle: powers are positions of trust, not permanent entitlements. Roles can be expanded, combined, or reassigned as the Federation grows.

How Appointments Work

The Founder may appoint active and reliable members to positions based on participation, judgment, and trust. As the region develops, more formal offices, titles, or internal procedures can be introduced.
